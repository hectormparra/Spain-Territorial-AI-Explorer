# Assignment 2 — 2–3 Minute Demo Video Script

**Recording requirement:** Record your own screen with the application running; this file is a script, not a recorded demo.

**0:00–0:20 — Introduction**  
"Our project is Spain Territorial AI Explorer. It supports SDG 11 by helping researchers explore population change and territorial indicators across Spanish municipalities."

**0:20–0:50 — Model and input**  
"The AI uses historical municipal population data available up to 2015. We compare Ridge Regression and Random Forest to estimate population growth between 2015 and 2025. We chose Random Forest because it had a lower test error."

**0:50–1:25 — Live municipality example**  
Select a municipality. "Here we select a municipality. The app displays its AI-estimated historical growth, observed growth, absolute error and a historical population chart. Housing and economic indicators are shown separately and were not used to train this model."

**1:25–1:50 — Comparison**  
Open Compare municipalities and choose two. "We can compare estimated and observed growth across municipalities."

**1:50–2:15 — Screening**  
Open Territorial screening and change a slider. "Users can filter municipalities by population, growth, rent and industry share, then download the results. This filter is rule-based, not an AI ranking."

**2:15–2:40 — Evaluation and limitations**  
Open Model evaluation. "On our held-out test, Random Forest achieved MAE 8.70 percentage points and R-squared 0.334. This is a historical demonstration, not a validated forecast of future growth. We disclose missing data, error and bias risks."

**2:40–2:50 — Closing**  
"The prototype works end to end. Future work will include transport accessibility, public services and out-of-time validation."

**Recording tips:** Use Windows Snipping Tool screen recording, OBS, or PowerPoint screen recording; capture the application and your voice. Verify sound and screen visibility before submitting.
